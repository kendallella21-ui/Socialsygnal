Questions, answered.

Is SYGNAL a watch?

Yes. The screen shows the time, and the light ring around it shows your mode.

How do I change modes?

Press the side button or rotate the band. The light ring switches between green, blue, and red instantly.

Does green mean I have to talk to anyone who approaches?

Never. Open means you're up for meeting people, not that you owe anyone your time. Switch to red whenever you like.

How does sharing info work?

Tap bands together to send a request in the SYGNAL app. Your details are shared only after both people allow it, and you can always decline.

Can my gym or venue run SYGNAL nights?

Yes. Choose the Host plan and we'll help you set up bands, signage, and the live room view.
