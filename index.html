Talk to us

Questions, answered.

Is SYGNAL a watch?

Yes. The face is a digital watch display that shows the time, while the light ring around it shows your mode.

How do I change modes?

Press the side button or rotate the band. The light ring switches between green, blue, and red instantly.

Does green mean I have to talk to anyone who approaches?

Never. Open means you're up for meeting people, not that you owe anyone your time. Switch to red whenever you like.

Do I need an app?

The band shows your signal on its own. The SYGNAL app is how you swap info: tap bands together, and your details only go through once each person allows it.

Can someone get my info without my permission?

No. Tapping bands only sends a request. Your details are shared only after you allow it in the app, and you can always decline.

Can my gym or venue run SYGNAL nights?

Yes. Choose the Host plan and we'll help you set up bands, signage, and the room dashboard.
